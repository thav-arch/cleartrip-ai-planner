Design concept · student project

AI trip planner

One trip that works for every person, and that every household agrees to. Type a trip in your own words and the plan is built from what you say.

On-device AIOff

Runs a small open model inside this browser tab. Nothing you type leaves your device, and there is no API key. The first load downloads about 1 GB.

  • Without the model, a scripted reader does the same job, so the prototype always works.
  • The rules decide what fits. The model only reads your message and explains the plan.
  • All trains, flights, stays and prices are made up. Nothing is booked or charged.

Not affiliated with Cleartrip. Built for a design assignment.
