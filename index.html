AI trip planner

One trip that works for every person, and that every household agrees to. Describe a trip in your own words, pick where from and where to, and ask the planner anything about the result.

Your planner’s personality
Which AI reads and explainsOff

  • The AI reads your message, explains the plan and answers questions. The rules decide what fits, so it can’t invent a stay or a price.
  • Stays and sights are real listings from OpenStreetMap. Fares, times and availability are estimates, and nothing is booked or charged here.
